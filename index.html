Course overview

From research question to working application.

The course covers the full empirical pipeline: framing economic questions, constructing datasets with AI methods, building reliable software, and reasoning about identification and inference.

Week 1

Methods and practical sessions

Online lectures on machine learning, text and image data, econometrics, LLMs, agents, and AI-assisted software development.

Week 2

Research and project studio

In-person faculty sessions at ETH Zurich, with structured time for project development, feedback, and advisor meetings.
